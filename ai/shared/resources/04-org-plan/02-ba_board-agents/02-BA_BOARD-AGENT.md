# Board Agents 
---

## Definition & Role

- The **Board Agents** sit at the top of the organisation just below NOA.
- The Board Agents act like the Board of Directors, providing strategic oversight, governance, and high-level decision-making for the entire agent ecosystem.
- Achieves a balance between **strategic oversight** and **operational agility** by keeping the Board Agents separate from execution details yet close enough to enforce policy.
- Each agent owns a domain e.g., (strategy, operations, finance, legal, security, partnerships, research)
- Their purpose is to translate NOA’s vision into specific missions, ensure alignment with  business model, and provide governance across all stacks and agents.

## Main responsibilities include:

  - Setting strategic direction and priorities
  - Top-level Advisors to NOA on domain-specific matters
  - Top reasoning authority for their domains
  - Tasks with knowledge-senthetic synthesis across multiple domains
  - Mermory and storage core for long-term institutional knowledge and Learning
